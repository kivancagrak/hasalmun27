Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile

Committee FIA — International Federation
Agenda The Future of Motorsports: Safety, Sustainability & Technology
Level Intermediate

The Fédération Internationale de l’Automobile serves as the global governing body for motorsports and mobility, overseeing everything from Formula 1 to road safety worldwide. In this committee, delegates will steer the future of the automobile itself — on the track and beyond.

From safety standards and sustainability to the technologies reshaping racing, each decision will echo across circuits and streets alike. Will you drive the sport into a new era, or stall at the grid?
