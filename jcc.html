Joint Crisis Committee

Committee JCC — Joint Crisis Committee
Agenda Classified — Revealed in Committee
Level Advanced

History analyzes wars, but rarely questions how differently they could have unfolded. What if the balance of power had tipped the other way? In the Joint Crisis Committee, opposing cabinets clash in real time — each decision made on one side of the map changing the fate of the other.

Alliances will be tested, fronts will collapse and peace will hang by a thread. There are no spectators in this committee — only victors and the vanquished. Which side of history will you write?
