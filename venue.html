Venue

Where the conference will come to life — in the heart of Istanbul.

Üsküdar University — Çarşı Campus

HASALMUN'27 will be held at the Çarşı Campus of Üsküdar University, one of Istanbul’s most dynamic universities, located in the historic district of Üsküdar on the Asian shore of the Bosphorus.

  • Çarşı Campus, Üsküdar University — Üsküdar / Istanbul, Türkiye
  • Easily reachable by Marmaray (Üsküdar Station), city ferries and municipal buses
  • All committee sessions, the opening ceremony and the closing ceremony will take place on campus
  • Cafes, restaurants and the Üsküdar seaside are within walking distance
