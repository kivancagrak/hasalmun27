Cabinet of Fidel Castro

Committee COFC — Crisis Cabinet
Agenda Classified — Revealed in Committee
Level Advanced

The Crisis Cabinet of Fidel Castro thrusts delegates into the heart of one of the most dangerous confrontations of the Cold War. As the revolution’s inner circle, delegates must defend the revolution at all costs — from enemies abroad and traitors within.

Cables will arrive, armies will move and the world will hold its breath. In this cabinet, secrecy is power and hesitation is defeat. Will you safeguard the revolution, or watch it burn?
