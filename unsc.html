United Nations Security Council

Committee UNSC — Principal Organ of the United Nations
Agenda Escalating Geopolitical Tensions & Military Buildups
Level Advanced

Geopolitical tensions are the defining challenge of our time. Escalating conflicts, military buildups and collapsing alliances have placed the world’s most powerful body at the center of the storm. In the United Nations Security Council, delegates will navigate crises where a single resolution — or a single veto — can change the fate of nations.

From proxy wars to nuclear brinkmanship, the agenda will demand diplomacy under pressure. Will you uphold international peace and security, or preside over the world’s descent into chaos?
