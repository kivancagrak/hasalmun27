International Civil Aviation Organization

Committee ICAO — Specialized Agency
Agenda Ensuring Safety, Efficiency & Cooperation in Global Aviation
Level Intermediate

The International Civil Aviation Organization committee places delegates at the center of global aviation governance. Within the International Civil Aviation Organization, ensuring safety, efficiency, and cooperation across international airspace is paramount. As air traffic increases and technology evolves, will nations unite under common standards, or allow division to create risk?

From safety regulations to environmental concerns and emerging technologies, each decision shapes the future of global travel. The skies connect the world — but will you keep them secure and unified, or let instability take flight?
