International Law Commission

Committee ILC — Expert Body of the United Nations
Agenda A Universal Framework for International Law
Level Advanced

Is a world united on international law possible — would justice be brought to all by a universal constitution? These are the questions at the heart of the International Law Commission, where delegates will attempt to codify the very framework that binds nations together.

From the law of treaties to the responsibility of states and the accountability of leaders, each decision will echo through the international order. Will you draft the constitution of a united world, or watch the ideal of universal justice dissolve into disagreement?
