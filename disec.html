GA-1: Disarmament & International Security Committee

Committee DISEC — First Committee of the General Assembly
Agenda Nuclear Disarmament & Non-Proliferation
Level Beginner — Recommended for First-Timers

The Disarmament and International Security Committee (GA-1: DISEC) stands as the first pillar of the United Nations General Assembly, tasked with safeguarding global peace through the control of armaments and the prevention of conflict.

From nuclear non-proliferation to the regulation of emerging weapon technologies, delegates will confront threats that no border can contain. Will you disarm a divided world, or watch the arms race spiral beyond control?
